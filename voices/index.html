GPTVoice

Your motion design videos finally have a voice

You make motion design, explainers or ads with your coding agent, and you are missing the voice-over. You already have a ChatGPT sign-in for Codex. Ask your agent for the voice: GPTVoice records it with OpenAI’s voices, with subtitles.

For video makers

From script to voiced video, without leaving your agent

  1. 01

    Write the script

    Or ask your agent to write it, scene by scene.

  2. 02

    Direct the voice

    Pick a voice, an emotion, a pace. Add [pause 1s] or [laughs] right in the text.

  3. 03

    Get audio and subtitles

    An MP3 or WAV plus an .srt file, read word for word.

  4. 04

    Drop it in your video

    In your editor, or straight into a code-based video (Remotion and the like) your agent is building.

Example request

Read this script as an upbeat explainer voice, coral, with a short pause between scenes. Save it to audio/voiceover.mp3 with subtitles.

Made for video: one clip per shot, fitted to its length

GPTVoice records sentence by sentence, so don’t ask for one long take. Give your agent the lines and the length of each shot: it generates one numbered clip per line, then looks at each clip and rewrites what doesn’t fit or doesn’t sound human.

  • generate_clips: one file per line (01-intro.mp3, 02-…), each with its subtitles and timings, plus a suggested timeline for the whole set.
  • Give each line a target length: the speed is adjusted and the take closest to the shot is kept (up to 3 tries). A line that can’t fit is flagged, so you shorten the text.
  • inspect_audio lets your agent “see” a clip: when each sentence starts and ends, every pause, the pace, the pitch, and a picture of the waveform.
  • Keep the same voice and settings for every clip (save them as a preset) so the clips match.

In our tests, 6 of 7 clips with 3 to 5 second targets landed within 0.21 s of their shot. The seventh, a whispered line, could not fit even at top speed and was flagged.

Under the hood

How GPTVoice gets your voices

The same sign-in as GPTImage, a different OpenAI model.

  1. 01

    The same sign-in

    GPTVoice reuses your GPTImage or Codex CLI sign-in (~/.gptimage/auth.json, ~/.codex/auth.json), or opens the official ChatGPT login once.

  2. 02

    OpenAI’s realtime voice model

    Your text is sent to wss://api.openai.com/v1/realtime, the model behind ChatGPT voice conversations, with directions for the voice, emotion and pace.

  3. 03

    Checked word for word

    The model’s own transcript is compared with your text. A passage that drifts is recorded again.

  4. 04

    Saved in your project

    The audio is joined, its levels evened out, then saved as MP3 or WAV, with optional subtitles.

Cost: unlike GPTImage, voice calls go to the personal OpenAI API organization linked to your account. They may be charged to its API credits or card (roughly $0.03–0.08 per minute of audio) and are not proven to be included in your ChatGPT plan. Check platform.openai.com/usage after your first try.

How it works, in detail

GPTVoice

Preview

Text in, voice out. A voice studio for Claude Code, with your ChatGPT sign-in.

Ask your agent for a narration, a voice-over or a dialogue, and get an MP3 or WAV back. Direct it like a voice actor: emotion, speed, whisper or shout, laughs and pauses right in your text. Every passage is checked word for word.

What it does

  • 10 voices, sorted by gender and character. Every voice speaks every language: the text decides.
  • Word-for-word reading: each passage is compared with your text and re-recorded if it drifts.
  • Made for video: one clip per shot, fitted to its length, and an inspector that lets your agent see each clip (sentence timings, pauses, pace, pitch, waveform picture).
  • Smooth, natural joins: whole sentences per take, soft crossfades and room tone instead of dead silence. Measured: no clicks or hard cuts between takes.
  • Dialogues with several voices in a single audio file.
  • Save your favorite voices and your own presets (voice + settings) to reuse them.
  • Subtitles (.srt) for your video editor, and transcription of any audio file.

How precise is each control

Exact

Applied to the audio itself, every time.

  • Speed, from 0.25× to 1.5×
  • Pitch shift, ±12 semitones
  • Silences to the millisecond: [pause 1s]

Strong

Clearly audible on almost every take.

  • Whisper or shout
  • Emotion and intensity
  • Narration styles: trailer, documentary, audiobook, ad, meditation…
  • Cues in your text: [whispers] [excited] [laughs] [sighs]

Best effort

Works often, not always.

  • Accent
  • Character voice (“an old sea captain”)
