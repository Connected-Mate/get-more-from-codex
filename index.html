Your ChatGPT subscription, inside your coding agent

You already pay for ChatGPT to use Codex. Two small open-source tools let Claude Code, Codex, Cursor or any MCP agent use that same sign-in to make images and voices. No API key to create, nothing new to subscribe to.

Make imagesMake voices
  • No API key
  • No extra subscription
  • Works with your coding agent
  • Open source (MIT)
Honest note.

This reuses the Codex “Sign in with ChatGPT” login. It is not an official OpenAI API: keep it personal. Images count against your ChatGPT plan; voice calls may be billed to your OpenAI API account. Read the details.

The idea

Plug your subscription into your agent

  1. 01

    Your subscription

    The ChatGPT plan you already use for Codex. Its sign-in can do more than code.

  2. 02

    An MCP plug

    GPTImage or GPTVoice signs in once, keeps the token on your computer, and turns it into tools your agent can call.

  3. 03

    Your agent creates

    Ask in plain words: “make a hero image”, “record the voice-over”. The file lands in your project.

Two tools

One for pictures, one for voices

GPTImage

Available

Logos, illustrations, mockups, banners, storyboards. Your agent makes them with GPT Image 2 and saves them in your project.

  • Counts against your ChatGPT plan
  • Works from your reference images
  • Never overwrites a file
Discover GPTImage →

GPTVoice

Preview

Voice-overs for motion design, explainers, ads and podcasts. 10 voices, emotions, whispers and laughs, subtitles included.

  • 10 voices, English and French
  • Read word for word
  • MP3 or WAV plus subtitles
Discover GPTVoice →
