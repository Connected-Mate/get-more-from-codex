## Design Context

### Users
Claude Code users (developers and non-technical makers) who already pay for ChatGPT and want images, voices and short films without an API bill. They land from GitHub or a shared link, skim on desktop or phone, and decide in under a minute whether to install.

### Brand Personality
Handmade, candid, cinematic. A pixel-art atelier: the GPTImage robot painter (cream body, gold joints, cyan screen face, purple beret) and its voice sibling (headphones, teal scarf).

### Aesthetic Direction
Mint paper + ink outlines + hard offset shadows, gold as the action color, cyan/teal as signal, purple only as a rare solid accent (links, never gradients). Light default; dark follows the system (deep ink-green, art shown in framed mint panels). Display: Pixelify Sans. Body: Atkinson Hyperlegible Next. Code: system monospace. Anti-refs: generic SaaS, purple gradients, Inter, identical card grids, invented metrics.

### Design Principles
1. Honesty up front: the grey-area notice is visible near the top, not buried.
2. Show, don't claim: real images made with GPTImage; GPTVoice shown as in development until real samples exist.
3. One accent at a time; ink outlines carry structure, no cards in cards.
4. Accessible by default: AA contrast both themes, keyboard, reduced motion, transcripts for audio.
5. FR and EN are equals: two real pages, same content.
