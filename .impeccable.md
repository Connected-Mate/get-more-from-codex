## Design Context

### Users
Claude Code users (developers and non-technical makers) who already pay for ChatGPT and want images, voices and short films without an API bill. They land from GitHub or a shared link, skim on desktop or phone, and decide in under a minute whether to install.

### Brand Personality
Quiet, candid, cinematic. A dusk-lit workspace (user-chosen art direction "Dimension", 05/10/2026). The pixel-art robots (GPTImage painter, GPTVoice singer) stay as the product art, always inside rounded frames.

### Aesthetic Direction
Dark only. Matte canvas #0a0a0a, graphite #161616 panels, frosted glass rgb(212 212 212 / .1) cards with 1px inset hairlines, pill buttons (white filled primary, hairline ghost), floating frosted nav. One warm-to-cool gradient (amber to cobalt) on the hero only. Type: DM Sans 500 for display (-0.035em) and UI, Geist for section headings. Text #ededed / #c2c2c2; #686868 is decoration only (fails AA as text). No offset shadows, no heavy borders.

### Design Principles
1. Honesty up front: the grey-area notice sits right under the hero.
2. Show, don't claim: real images and real audio; no unverified numbers.
3. Restraint: weight 500, achromatic UI, color only in the hero gradient and the art.
4. Accessible by default: AA contrast, keyboard, reduced motion and transparency, transcripts.
5. FR and EN are equals: two real pages, proper French typography.
